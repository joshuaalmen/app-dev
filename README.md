# My Favorite Movie: Avengers: Endgame

![Avengers: Endgame Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/0d/Avengers_Endgame_poster.jpg)
**Avengers: Endgame** is a superhero movie produced by *Marvel Studios* and directed by **Anthony and Joe Russo**.  
It’s the final chapter of the *Infinity Saga*, where the Avengers unite to defeat **Thanos** and save the universe.
- **Iron Man (Tony Stark)**
- **Captain America (Steve Rogers)**
- **Thor**
- **Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff)**
- **Hulk (Bruce Banner)**
> I love this movie because it’s emotional, action-packed, and shows teamwork and sacrifice.  
> The ending made me cry but also inspired me.
> "Whatever it takes." – Captain America
